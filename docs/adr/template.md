# NNNN. Title, as a decision not a topic

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by 00NN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces in play. What made a decision necessary. Constraints that were real
at the time, not ones invented to justify the answer.

## Decision

What we do, in the present tense. One paragraph.

## Consequences

**Good:** what this buys.

**Cost:** what it makes harder or impossible. Be specific — this is the section
a future reader actually needs.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and the single reason each was not chosen.

## Revisit if

The observable condition that would make this decision wrong.
